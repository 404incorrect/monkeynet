MonkeyNet · The Canopy Fund

MonkeyGives

For the monkeys in the real canopy.

We spend a lot of time treating the canopy as a metaphor. For the monkeys below, it's an actual forest, and it's getting harder to live in. These are real organizations doing the real work: pulling monkeys out of the pet trade and shuttered labs, cutting snares out of the forest, and giving the ones who can't go home somewhere good to land. Every name links straight out to them. We take nothing.

How these were chosen. Independent transparency first — Candid (GuideStar) seals and Charity Navigator accountability scores — then a short, honest line between a donation and a monkey. They're ordered roughly by how strong that line is, not by a score we invented. No affiliate links, no sponsorships, nothing paid to be here. If one loses its footing, we drop it.

Rescue & sanctuary

For the monkey who can't go back to the wild — a good place to land instead.

🇺🇸 Sanctuary · Kentucky, USA

Primate Rescue Center

Candid Platinum Seal · Charity Navigator 100% accountability & finance

A lifelong home for monkeys (and a few apes) rescued from the pet trade, roadside zoos, and research — with about as clean a transparency record as this field has. If you want the shortest, clearest path from a dollar to a cared-for monkey, start here.

Donate → primaterescue.org
🌍 Sanctuary network · 13 African countries

Pan African Sanctuary Alliance (PASA)

Candid Gold Seal · Charity Navigator rated · 23 member sanctuaries

Not one sanctuary but the alliance holding up twenty-three of them across Africa — feeding orphaned and confiscated monkeys and apes, funding emergency care, and training the next generation of African conservationists. If you can give only once and want the widest reach, this is the branch.

Donate → Charity Navigator ↗
🇺🇸 Sanctuary · Texas, USA + Zambia

Born Free USA

Candid Platinum Seal · Charity Navigator 3★

Runs one of America's largest primate sanctuaries — 175 acres in south Texas for monkeys retired from labs and the pet trade — and rescues and releases vervet monkeys back into the wild through its project in Zambia. Rescue on one continent, release on another.

Donate → bornfreeusa.org
🇬🇧 Sanctuary · Cornwall, UK

Wild Futures — The Monkey Sanctuary

UK registered charity · rescuing monkeys since 1964

One of Europe's longest-running primate rescues, home to monkeys taken in from the UK pet trade and beyond, and a steady voice against keeping primates as pets. The UK-side option if that's closer to home for you.

Donate → wildfutures.org
Out in the forest

For keeping wild monkeys where they belong — in the canopy, uncaught.

🌏 Conservation · Vietnam & Cambodia

Douc Langur Foundation

Candid Platinum Seal · Charity Navigator 86% accountability & finance

The douc langur is one of the most striking monkeys alive and one of the most snared. This foundation pulls traps out of the forest, gets individuals out of illegal markets, and studies a species we still barely understand. High transparency, very direct work.

Donate → douclangur.org
🌎 Conservation · Peru, Colombia & Argentina

Neotropical Primate Conservation

~85% of income to programs · community-run reserves

Works with local communities to set up forest reserves and wildlife corridors, replant what's been cut, and return rescued howler monkeys to the trees. Lean, field-first, and most of what you give lands directly on the work.

Donate → neoprimate.org

A note on the cousins: apes aren't monkeys, but they're up in the same trees, and the best-rated primate charity going is the Jane Goodall Institute (chimpanzees) — with the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund close behind. If your heart is with the great apes, give there instead.

The canopy we talk about is a metaphor. Theirs is a real forest. Hold up a piece of it.
