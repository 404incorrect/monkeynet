The ClimbLog

Dispatches from this canopy, the one you're standing in.

MonkeyNews covers monkeys out in the world. This covers the site itself: every branch it has grabbed since the first one. The vine roots at the bottom, on the day MonkeyNet found something to hold onto, and grows up from there. Read it however you like, but it was built to be climbed.

  1. July 9, 2026

    Weather came to the canopy

    The sky over the hub now drifts with your own clock: dawn, day, dusk, night. Sometimes it storms, and when it storms, it rains bananas. Nobody asked for this.

  2. July 9, 2026

    The Canopy Count started keeping score

    A hit counter, the way the old web kept track of who came by. Every visit is one swing. The number only goes up, which is the whole point of a number like that.

  3. July 9, 2026

    The shrine stopped sticking

    Candles at MonkeyShrine would freeze mid-flicker, and the video refused to be scrubbed. Both let go. Harambe's candle burns properly now.

  4. July 9, 2026

    MonkeyMaker grew a color wheel and 91 stickers

    Text fill and outline opened up to the whole spectrum, the sticker drawer went from a handful to ninety-one, and the canvas quit blowing itself out on phones.

  5. July 6, 2026

    MonkeyDraw folded into MonkeyMaker

    Two studios became one, with a single layer stack underneath. You can still draw on top of a monkey, or start from nothing at all. Nothing was lost in the merge.

  6. July 3, 2026

    MonkeyShrine opened

    A quiet page, a candle, a place to leave a banana. It has been ten years and it still sits wrong. Light one if you need to.

  7. July 2, 2026

    MonkeyNews began running the Canopy Wire

    Real dispatches on real monkeys out in the world, every one of them sourced and linked back to the humans who reported it first.

  8. July 2, 2026

    A paint pad appeared, and the Gallery took hand drawings

    MonkeyDraw arrived and wired itself straight into the wall. Draw a monkey, hang it up, no account, no algorithm, no permission.

  9. June 30, 2026

    The hero picture started cycling monkeys

    Pixel monkeys took over the cards, the palette softened, and the front page stopped standing still.

  10. June 29, 2026

    MonkeyNet found a branch and held on

    The first climb. One page, one idea, and a strong suspicion that the internet was supposed to be a canopy the whole time.
